# Smart DentalOps – Integrated Dental Practice Intelligence System

## Problem Statement

Design and build an integrated intelligent dental practice system that optimizes the complete patient–practice relationship lifecycle, improving operational efficiency, patient engagement, and financial performance.

The proposed solution must address at least one major core challenge, while maintaining scalability, real-time adaptability, and business feasibility.

# Core Challenge Areas

# 1. Smart Scheduling Optimization

Develop an intelligent scheduling engine capable of maximizing appointment efficiency while adapting dynamically to real-world dental clinic operations.

## Functional Requirements

### Schedule Optimization Engine must consider:

- Provider productivity patterns  
  - Dentist  
  - Registered Hygienist  
  - Certified Dental Therapist  

- Dental procedure complexity and dependencies  
- Chair utilization metrics  
- Historical appointment adherence behavior  
- Emergency slot allocation  

## Real-Time Schedule Adjustment Support

System must dynamically respond to:

- Last-minute cancellations  
- Procedure complications  
- Emergency appointments  
- Provider availability changes  

## Expected Intelligence Layer

- Predict no-show probability  
- Suggest best appointment slot automatically  
- Reassign unused chair time  
- Optimize provider idle time  

# 2. Case Acceptance Acceleration

Build a digital system that improves treatment plan acceptance and reduces delay in patient decision-making.

## Functional Requirements

- Dynamic treatment plan presentation  
- Financial option optimization  
- Automated follow-up workflows  
- Digital consent management  
- Progress tracking and treatment visualization  

## Real-Time Analytics Required

- Acceptance rate trends  
- Financial option effectiveness  
- Provider presentation success rates  

## Expected Intelligence Layer

- Predict treatment acceptance probability  
- Recommend best financing option  
- Trigger follow-up sequences automatically
